# Stage 6 portal and QA entry decisions

Status: **RECOMMENDED DEFAULTS PREPARED / STAGE 5 AND FOUNDER GATED**

This record begins only after full Bid-to-Won and stable Stage 4/5 agreement,
invoice and visit ownership. R3-5 C0 remains the accepted token/session and
proposal-receipt foundation; Stage 6 does not recreate it.

## Recommended first-build defaults

| Decision | Recommended default | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Portal identity | Separate customer stakeholder identity and assignment; never plaintext proposal tracking ID | PENDING |
| Scope | Explicit account, location, object and document grants with uniform denial and portal audit | PENDING |
| First portal value | Account/document home, accepted agreements/change orders, upcoming/completed visits and bounded requests | PENDING |
| Billing visibility | Read-only only where accepted Stage 4 capability and customer scope exist | PENDING |
| Messaging | Transactional request threads only; no inferred marketing consent | PENDING |
| Attachments | Private, scanned, retention-bound and separately customer-visible; no access notes/internal evidence | PENDING |
| QA pilot | Ordinary-work manual inspection templates and internal results first | PENDING |
| QA completion | Versioned scoring, critical overrides, deficiencies, corrective actions, re-service, recurrence and closure | PENDING |
| Evidence | Content hash, visibility, EXIF/redaction state, retention and immutable provenance | PENDING |
| Safety | Incident/hazard stop-and-route only; no regulated classification or clearance | PENDING |

## Required proof

- stakeholder assignment/revocation, shared inbox, forwarding and full IDOR
  matrices;
- purpose/expiry/session/rate-limit and current-role reauthorization;
- customer-safe projection with cost, wage, margin, access/internal/provider and
  worker-private fields excluded;
- attachment scan, retention, deletion, visibility and cross-record evidence
  substitution tests;
- scoring/critical-override/deficiency/corrective/re-service/closure invariants;
- accessibility, genuine 390px, customer/operator validation and independent
  review; and
- explicit proof that legacy plaintext tracking and mutable accepted status are
  never portal identity or acceptance authority.
