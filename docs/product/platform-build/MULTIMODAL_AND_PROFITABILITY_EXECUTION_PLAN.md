walkthroughs faster and more complete, connects only approved observations to
Veltex's deterministic estimating engine, and later shows estimated,
scheduled and actual operating performance without representing Veltex as an
accounting, payroll, tax, legal or safety-certification system.

## Governing rules

1. Build the smallest secure functional release at each milestone.
2. Do not reopen R3-1 or replace the existing pricing engine.
3. Human confirmation is mandatory before AI output changes scope, risk state,
   estimate inputs or proposal evidence.
4. Originals, derivatives, transcripts, suggestions and decisions are private,
   tenant-bound records. Customer visibility is a separate explicit choice.
5. No raw media or protected content enters logs, analytics, audit/outbox
   payloads or unreviewed customer documents.
6. No paid AI call, provider credential, hosted upload, deployment or external
   message occurs without its applicable action-specific approval.
