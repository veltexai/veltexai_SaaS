# Stage 7 carpet-first service-pack gate

Status: **GATE PACKET PREPARED / STAGES 1–6 AND EVIDENCE BLOCKED**

Carpet cleaning is the first ordinary-specialty candidate. This packet does not
claim that legacy carpet recognition or generic specialty taxonomy is an
accepted service pack.

## Required evidence before implementation

- Carpet-specific B18/E2+ research with a distinct qualified operator cohort.
- Explicit ordinary boundary: cleaning only; no regulated contamination,
  restoration, mold/asbestos/lead, hazardous materials or damage certification.
- Measurement model, fiber/surface questions, visible-condition limits,
  equipment methods, drying/access assumptions and operator override rules.
- Evidence-backed productivity ranges, materials/consumables, equipment or
  rental, travel/mobilization, minimum charge and uncertainty treatment.
- Founder-approved service name, intake questions, scope, exclusions,
  responsibilities, add-ons, safety flags and truthful sales language.

## Required implementation shape

1. Additive persisted catalog/version record; taxonomy literals are not the
   authoritative pack.
2. Strict carpet-specific input schema and accessible conditional UI.
3. Deterministic low/base/high pricing using the accepted v2 engine contract.
4. Customer-safe scope/proposal renderer plus low/normal/high fixtures.
5. Frozen snapshot/version provenance; no mutation of existing five-service or
   legacy outputs.
6. Segment/service analytics only after acceptance; sample/test excluded.

## Required release proof

- exact calculation fixtures and boundary/invalid/hazard negatives;
- save/edit/regenerate/print/send/download and subscription regressions;
- existing five v2 job types and legacy-golden adapter remain byte/behavior
  compatible where frozen;
- desktop/390px keyboard, labels, focus, overflow and contrast evidence;
- qualified carpet-operator validation, exact Claude PASS and founder
  acceptance; and
- separately authorized Preview/production enablement and truthful onboarding/
  marketing activation only after acceptance.

## Later independent gates

Upholstery and tile/grout each require their own evidence and pack after carpet.
Hard-floor care requires a separately reviewed chemistry/equipment boundary.
Window cleaning is limited to ground-level/interior ordinary work. Ordinary
post-construction is final cleaning with explicit exclusions. Elevated exterior,
roof/gutter and pressure/soft-wash services remain outside this gate.
