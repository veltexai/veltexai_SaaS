# Veltex brand architecture and validation plan

Status: **APPROVED / ACTIVE / NO FULL REBRAND AUTHORIZED**
Founder approval: 2026-10-03 Pacific

## Decision

Use `Veltex` as the primary customer-facing master brand, describe the category
as `The cleaning business operating system`, and present AI as a transparent,
operator-controlled supporting capability:

> Veltex  
> The cleaning business operating system  
> AI-assisted estimating, walkthroughs, proposals, and profitability insights.

Keep `veltexai.com`, existing accounts, email addresses, provider projects,
legal/product references and the current `Veltex AI` operational identity for
now. This is progressive positioning, not a technical or legal rename.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Master brand:** Veltex
2. **Category:** Cleaning Business Operating System
3. **Primary promise:** Run your cleaning business from walkthrough to profit.
4. **Technology proof:** AI-assisted where it saves time; the operator controls
   scope, price and final decisions.
5. **Trust proof:** deterministic calculations, editable outputs, private
   customer information, visible assumptions and a fully usable manual path.

The product must remain valuable during an AI-provider outage or when an
organization disables an AI feature.

## Claims and language rules

Approved themes:

- `AI-assisted`, `operator-reviewed`, `editable` and `reviewable`;
- faster evidence organization and proposal preparation;
- visible pricing assumptions and deterministic calculations;
- private organization-scoped information; and
- operational insights based on the business's own inputs.

Prohibited themes:

- “AI runs your cleaning company” or replaces employees/judgment;
- perfect or fully automatic pricing;
- guaranteed profit, accuracy, compliance or business success;
- hidden AI use;
- generic chatbot/model terminology as the primary sales message; and
- any claim that Veltex is accounting, payroll, tax, legal or safety software.

## Phased implementation

### B0 — inventory and freeze

- Inventory customer-visible `Veltex AI`, logo, metadata, email, document,
  social, provider and legal references.
- Classify each as master-brand copy, legal/operational identifier, historical
  evidence or provider-bound configuration.
- Do not bulk-replace strings. Domains, email identities, database names,
  provider projects and historical evidence stay unchanged.
- Record the current production conversion baseline before testing variants.

### B1 — message system

- Create one approved messaging dictionary and reusable UI/content constants.
- New or materially revised public surfaces lead with `Veltex`; an honest
  `AI-assisted` explanation remains visible where the capability matters.
- Product screens emphasize operator control, privacy and transparent math.
- Existing contractual documents retain their current legal identity until a
  separate legal/trademark decision.

### B2 — bounded buyer validation

Test identical features, pricing, screenshots and calls to action with only the
brand/category framing changed:

- **A:** `Veltex AI — AI Operating System for Cleaning Companies`
- **B:** `Veltex — Cleaning Business Operating System`
- **C:** `Veltex — Cleaning Business Software with AI-Assisted Estimating`

Recruit real commercial, residential, turnover, specialty and small multi-crew
cleaning operators. Segment results by company size and AI familiarity.

Measure:

- trust and fastest correct understanding;
- qualified demo/trial intent and completed qualified signup;
- willingness to upload private walkthrough photos;
- confidence in pricing outputs;
- privacy, job-replacement and automation concerns; and
- unaided recall after 24–48 hours.

Clicks alone do not decide the brand. Interviews must capture why a variant was
trusted or rejected.

### B3 — decision gate

- Retain `Veltex AI` prominently if it performs equivalently or better and
  causes no repeated trust objection.
- Move public presentation toward `Veltex` if outcome-led positioning improves
  qualified conversion by at least 10–15% relative and interviews consistently
  identify `AI` in the name as the obstacle.
- If results are mixed, use `Veltex` as the prominent wordmark while retaining
  `Veltex AI` legally and operationally during a gradual transition.
- Require professional trademark clearance before material investment in a
  Veltex-only national identity. Web/USPTO keyword searches are preliminary,
  not legal clearance.

### B4 — controlled rollout

Only after B3 approval:

1. update shared customer-facing brand constants and approved public copy;
2. verify metadata, accessibility names, emails, PDFs, proposal/tracking pages,
   analytics and support language;
3. preserve domain redirects and the existing operational identity;
4. release behind an observable, reversible rollout;
5. monitor qualified conversion, trust objections, support contacts and
   unsubscribe/bounce behavior; and
6. retain an immediate copy rollback without changing customer data.

## Research interpretation

The approved recommendation is intentionally cautious. General-public research
shows material AI concern, while B2B research also associates AI software with
innovation and favors clear disclosure. Small-business adoption and readiness
vary. No credible study proves that `AI` in a software name helps or harms
purchase intent among cleaning-company owners specifically. Veltex therefore
tests its actual target buyers instead of treating broad surveys as a naming
verdict.

Research sources preserved with the founder-approved recommendation:

- Pew Research Center, 2025 public attitudes toward AI;
- Pew public/expert risk comparison;
- Ipsos B2B AI-software research;
- NFIB 2025 Small Business and Technology Survey; and
- U.S. Census small-business AI adoption research.

## Release and action gates

- No full rebrand, domain migration, email migration, provider rename, trademark
  filing, paid study, advertisement, publication or campaign mutation is
  authorized by this plan alone.
- Research recruitment and external interviews require an approved script,
  privacy notice, incentive budget and action-specific authorization.
- Production copy experiments require baseline instrumentation, feature/variant
  assignment, accessibility review, exact deployment review and founder
  approval.
- Every rejected variant and reason remains in the operating ledger so failed
  positioning is not silently retried.

## Completion criteria

This program is complete only when the three variants have comparable target-
buyer evidence, the B3 rule produces an explicit founder decision, any selected
rollout passes legal/trademark and production gates, and all customer-facing
surfaces truthfully preserve operator control and transparent deterministic
pricing.
