# Stage 6 customer portal and quality-assurance readiness

Status: READ-ONLY AUDIT COMPLETE / EXPANDED STAGE NOT STARTED

Authoritative audit head: `fdf64ab`

No branch contains a hidden portal-identity, stakeholder-scope, inspection,
deficiency, corrective-action or re-service implementation. Existing proposal
tracking is a prototype delivery/read model, not a secure portal foundation.

## C0 belongs to Stage 2

The C0 proposal room is a Bid-to-Won dependency and must be completed before an
expanded portal:

- Hash-only CSPRNG action tokens with purpose, object-version, expiry, rotation,
  revocation and an explicit legacy-link window.
- Immutable proposal version and acceptance artifact.
- Review, ask, request changes, decline and `Accept proposal` actions plus a
  receipt.
- Designated approver, typed name/consent, approved wording version, document
  hash and timestamp.
- Explicit customer read projection excluding costs, wages, margins, internal
  notes, access codes, provider IDs and worker data.
- Tenant/IDOR, forwarding/shared-inbox, rate-limit and accessibility proof.

The current tracking migrations/routes and public proposal renderer may inform
the narrow RPC and customer-safe projection patterns, but plaintext tracking
IDs, mutable proposals and operator-set accepted status cannot be promoted into
C0 acceptance evidence.

## Expanded portal C1–C7

- Separate portal identity and customer-stakeholder assignments.
- Account, location, object and document scopes with portal audit.
- Account/document home, agreement/change-order history, visits/evidence,
  issues/requests/messages, renewals and preferences.
- Billing visibility only after the relevant Stage 4 capability exists.
- Consent/suppression registry and transactional-versus-marketing enforcement.
- Retention, export, deletion and attachment-privacy rules.

## Quality assurance Q1+

- Versioned inspection templates/items and assignments.
- Responses, scoring, coverage and critical-item overrides.
- Deficiencies, corrective actions, re-service, recurrence and closure evidence.
- Evidence hash, EXIF handling, redaction, internal/customer visibility and
  retention.
- Minimal incident/hazard stop-and-route plus immutable audit.

## Entry and release gates

- Accepted R2 tenancy and accepted R3 C0/immutable proposal ownership.
- Stable agreement and visit ownership from Stages 4/5.
- Privacy, retention, customer-acceptance wording and attachment decisions.
- B12 evidence for expanded portal demand and B13 evidence that inspections and
  quality-linked loss justify QA.
- WCAG 2.2 AA target evidence, role/IDOR negatives, operator/customer validation,
  Claude exact-candidate PASS and founder acceptance.
- QA begins with ordinary Tier O. Regulated/high-hazard inspection and incident
  semantics remain separately blocked.

## First bounded slices

1. After R2 acceptance, implement only the Stage 2 token foundation: additive
   schema/service, hash-only storage, legacy compatibility, resolution and
   tenant/IDOR tests. Do not add acceptance UI yet.
2. After R3 immutable proposal versions, implement C0 review/accept/receipt.
3. Only after B13 and stable visits/contracts, pilot Q1 internally for ordinary
   work with manual templates. Exclude customer publication, photos, automated
   re-service and safety classification from the first Q1 slice.
