# 30-day Reel-led launch plan

Produce twelve original units in four weekly filming blocks. Each unit becomes a distinct Instagram Reel, Facebook Reel, YouTube Short, and—when suitable—a slower, credibility-led LinkedIn native video or document carousel. These are placements, not four new ideas.

| Week | Theme | Original units |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cost truth | Bid Teardown: “This bid looked profitable until this line”; Missing Number: “Your wage is not your labor cost”; Build With Me: walkthrough notes to estimate |
| 2 | Scope clarity | Scope Words: why “general cleaning” is dangerous; Walkthrough: what to count before quoting restrooms; Bid Teardown: same building, two assumptions |
| 3 | Product and proposal | Build With Me: scope to proposal; Missing Number: amortizing periodic floor care; Owner Q&A from a real approved comment |
| 4 | Proof and offer | Bid Teardown: margin versus markup; Scope Words: exclusions that prevent scope creep; Founder POV: why Veltex AI offers a free calculator |

## Production requirements

- Use readable first-frame text before audio begins.
- Change the visual every two to four seconds without unnecessary effects.
- Use real product screens, original facility footage, annotated numbers, and founder delivery.
- Burn in accurate captions and keep important text inside safe areas.
- Use commercially licensed music only; speech must remain clear.
- Create three hook variants before filming. Select one manually for the pilot; retain the variants for later controlled testing.
- Never display customer names, addresses, facility security details, or identifiable bid documents.

## Native placement rules

- Instagram: concise caption; link in bio or Story sticker; prioritize saves and shares.
- Facebook: Reel plus a clear calculator link in caption; prioritize reach and link clicks.
- LinkedIn: slower native-video cut or document carousel; put the link in the first comment; prioritize substantive comments and profile visits.
- YouTube: question-matched title and description, `#Shorts`, calculator link in description and pinned comment; prioritize viewed-versus-swiped, retention, and subscribers gained.

## Decision windows

- Creative: compare only after 1,500 reach per variant.
- Topic/offer: compare only after 40 attributable landing sessions per series.
- Trial-to-paid: evaluate only after 25 trials in a monthly cohort.
- Channel go/no-go: day 90, not day 30.
