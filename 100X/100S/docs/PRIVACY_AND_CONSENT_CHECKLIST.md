# Privacy and consent launch checklist

This engineering checklist is not legal advice. A qualified privacy professional should review the production configuration before pixels, audiences, or automated outreach are enabled.

- Keep campaign targeting US-only and do not upload purchased, scraped, or sensitive-personal-data audiences.
- Confirm the privacy notice names each enabled analytics or advertising provider, the purposes, retention, and opt-out routes.
- Verify consent and Global Privacy Control behavior before enabling nonessential browser pixels in jurisdictions where required.
- Use first-party UTM attribution with a 90-day maximum; never place names, email addresses, phone numbers, or customer identifiers in UTM values.
- Keep the calculator estimate email strictly transactional. Do not add recipients to marketing without separate, recorded consent.
- Hash rate-limit identifiers, define retention for raw funnel events, and restrict service-role access.
- Configure data-processing terms and least-privilege access for every social platform and measurement provider.
- Provide an accessible unsubscribe mechanism for any future marketing email and preserve suppression records.
- Document deletion/export procedures for attribution and engagement records tied to a user.
- Re-run this review before international expansion; do not treat the US configuration as sufficient elsewhere.
