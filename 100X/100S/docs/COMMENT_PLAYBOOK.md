# Comment and message playbook

| Class | SLA | AI draft | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead or product question | 4 business hours | Allowed | Human verifies and approves a helpful answer; move private/account details to a secure channel |
| Objection | Same business day | Allowed | Answer the concern without attacking competitors or promising outcomes |
| Complaint | 1 hour when monitored | Prohibited | Human acknowledges, moves account details private, and escalates |
| Legal/employment/privacy | Immediate escalation | Prohibited | Do not answer publicly beyond acknowledging receipt |
| Spam | 24 hours | Prohibited | Human hides/reports when appropriate; do not engage |
| General question | Same business day | Allowed | Answer from current approved product documentation |

Never answer publicly with contract guarantees, customer-specific information, individual bid recommendations, legal or employment advice, refund decisions, security details, or unsupported competitor comparisons. Do not collect passwords, payment information, or private facility data in social messages.

Initial monitoring windows: 7–9am and 5–9pm Eastern on publishing days, with a named owner and backup. Adjust only from observed audience response times.
