# 100S one-week manual pilot

## Preconditions

- Apply reviewed migrations in an approved nonproduction environment first; record the migration ledger entry `100s_001` separately from the app migration sequence.
- Populate `operator/accounts.json` only after confirming Veltex ownership, professional/business status, analytics access, and least-privilege roles.
- Keep `publishingEnabled` and `commentReplyEnabled` false.
- Name the on-call human and backup. If there is only one reviewer, require a 30-minute cooling-off period plus the approval checklist.
- Produce original founder/product/facility footage and use commercially licensed audio.

## Approval checklist

1. Every factual claim maps to a current verified claim.
2. Hook, voiceover, on-screen text, caption, CTA, first comment, thumbnail, and destination passed the current content-hash verdict.
3. The human can defend every number and would send the statement to a customer.
4. Captions are accurate; UI/customer information is not visible; music is licensed for commercial use.
5. Platform account and placement are correct. Publishing remains manual.

## Pilot

Publish three original creative units across Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts, plus a native LinkedIn placement. Record provider post IDs manually. Do not automate comments.

## Day-seven pass rule

- Three original units reached all three short-video surfaces.
- Median hook rate (3-second views / reach) is at least 25%.
- At least one unit reaches 10 saves + shares per 1,000 reach.
- At least 30 social-attributed calculator sessions.
- Zero compliance incidents and zero unapproved publishes.

Trials are recorded but are not a seven-day pass condition. Do not make trial-to-paid decisions before a cohort contains at least 25 trials; evaluate channel viability at day 90.

## Kill switch

Stop publishing immediately for any unapproved publish, material factual correction, account warning, credential concern, or public complaint mishandling. Set the campaign inactive and investigate before resuming.
